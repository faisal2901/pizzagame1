Pizza Empire

Bake, serve and race your family to the top.

Who are you?
How to play. Drag anywhere to walk. Take dough from the mixer to an oven, carry hot pizzas to the counter, and stand at the till to sell. Collect cash, clean tables for tips, and box pizzas for delivery orders.

Empire. Spend cash on the dashed pads for tables, ovens and staff. Keep customers happy to raise your star rating, beat live challenges, and open branches from Hyderabad to New York.

Pizza Battle. Everyone works the same shop for 4 minutes and every action scores points. Live challenges pay a bonus to whoever wins them. Empty spots are filled by bots, so you can practise alone.

Online. One person creates a room and shares the 4-letter code. Up to 4 phones, anywhere, on Wi-Fi or mobile data.
